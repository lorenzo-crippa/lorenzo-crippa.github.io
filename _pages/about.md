Welcome! I am a Lecturer (Assistant Professor) in Political Economy at the [University of Strathclyde](https://www.strath.ac.uk/staff/crippalorenzodr/) in Glasgow, where I am also affiliated with the local branch of the [Global Environmental Measurement and Monitoring (GEMM) Initiative](https://www.strath.ac.uk/workwithus/globalenvironmentalmeasurementmonitoring/) and with the [PLACE lab for computational social sciences](https://www.strath.ac.uk/humanities/theplacelab/).

My research interests, in the field of international political economy, primarily concern the global governance of firms. I examine how firms contribute to global challenges (like climate change, corruption, illicit finance) and explore cross-border policy solutions to address these issues. In this sense, I have studied state regulations, international law and organizations, and market-based or voluntary initiatives. Methodologically, I primarily apply computational methods for causal inference.

Some of my research has appeared or is forthcoming in peer-reviewed journals such as _The Journal of Politics_, _International Organization_, and _Political Science Research & Methods_. My research has been funded, among others, by the British Academy/Leverhulme Trust and the Royal Society of Edinburgh. 

Alongside peer-reviewed publications, I have engaged in policy-relevant research and knowledge exchange. I have contributed to outputs for institutions such as the World Bank, for outlets like _Foreign Affairs_, and disseminated my research with institutions such as the OECD or the Westminster Foundation for Democracy.
